# StrikAll | Interactive Entertainment Platform

**Jogue. Acelere. Divirta-se.**

StrikAll is an interactive entertainment website designed to bring bowling, kart racing, themed food, and recreational experiences together in one digital platform.

The project combines a bold retro-inspired visual identity with interactive elements, attraction showcases, pricing information, reservation flows, and location details, creating a digital experience that reflects the excitement of the StrikAll entertainment center.

## Overview

The website was designed to serve as the company's main digital touchpoint, allowing visitors to explore its attractions, discover available experiences, check prices, plan visits, and interact with the reservation interface.

Its design combines dark backgrounds, vivid orange and purple accents, expressive typography, and immersive visual elements inspired by arcade culture and the 1980s.

## Features

### Landing Page
A visually immersive homepage featuring the StrikAll brand, mascot, slogan, and main calls to action, introducing visitors to the entertainment experience.

### Attractions
Dedicated content showcasing the available activities:

- **Bowling** — recreational bowling experiences for friends, families, and groups.
- **Kart** — racing experiences focused on speed, competition, and adrenaline.
- **Themed Food** — a food experience complementing the entertainment environment.

### Pricing & Combos
A dedicated pricing section presenting the costs of attractions, food, and promotional combos, helping customers explore the available experiences and plan their visit.

### Interactive Reservations
A reservation interface designed to collect customer information and preferences, including the selected experience, date, time, and participant details.

### Track Challenge
An interactive section dedicated to the kart experience, featuring track-related visuals and route information.

### Location
A location section featuring geographic information and map visualization, helping customers find the entertainment center and plan their route.

### Customer Experience
Interactive elements, navigation, modal interfaces, and calls to action designed to create a more intuitive and engaging journey throughout the website.

## Design System

The website follows StrikAll's visual identity, combining retro aesthetics with a modern digital interface.

| Color | Hex |
|---|---|
| StrikAll Orange | `#FD6500` |
| Vivid Purple | `#520AE1` |

The visual language is built around contrast, energy, movement, speed, and entertainment, supported by neon-inspired details and arcade-style elements.
